About this app

Internal GPS Info is a motorsport GPS meter for Android. It measures speed, acceleration (0–60 and ¼-mile), roll-on, braking and lap times, records and replays sessions, and saves locations — all from your phone's built-in GPS, barometer and accelerometer, with no external hardware.

No extra hardware. Everything runs on the phone's built-in GNSS receiver, barometer and accelerometer. Because Android GPS performance varies a lot between phones, a built-in GPS Capabilities check measures what your device actually delivers — update rate, dual-frequency (L5) reception, satellites and accuracy — so you know what to expect.

What's inside

A full motorsport toolkit, built for the track and private land.

Live GPS data

Big speed readout plus position, accuracy, satellites, barometer-fused elevation, heading, g-force and UTC time.

Performance timers

0–60 / ¼-mile standing runs, roll-on, braking, and 0–V–0 with a brake cue. Trap speeds, best times, and estimated power.

Lap timer

Drop a start/finish line and sector gates on the map. Sector splits, a live delta to your best lap, and a theoretical-best lap.

Session recording

Record a run, then replay it on a speed-coloured map with charts, and export to GPX or CSV.

Saved places & compass

Save spots with full GPS detail, view them on a map, and get a live compass that points back to any saved location.

Garage & power

Keep your cars with weight and drivetrain (268 presets built in). Runs log per car with an estimated peak power.

Installing

It's a direct download (not on the Play Store yet), so you'll allow a one-time install from your browser. Takes about a minute.

Download the APK

On your Android phone, tap Download for Android above to get GPS-Info.apk.

Open the file

Tap it in your download notification, or find it in Files › Downloads and tap to open.

Allow the install

Android will ask to allow installs from your browser — tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, go back, then Install. If Play Protect warns, choose Install anyway.

Open & allow location

Launch Internal GPS Info, accept the safety notice, and grant precise location. Head outside for a clear sky.

Tip: run GPS Capabilities (the ⋮ menu on the Live screen) outdoors first to see your phone's update rate and accuracy — it sets expectations for the timers and lap timing.

Drive responsibly. Speed, acceleration, braking and lap features are for a closed course, track, or private land only, with a qualified driver — never on public roads. Have a passenger operate the phone, and obey all laws. The readings are estimates.

Ready to run?

Grab the latest build and take it to the track. Free, no account, works offline once installed.
